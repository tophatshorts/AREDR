BEHIND THE REEL

Luck, explained.

Rolls select from the downloaded rated-level pool. Each level has weight (0.65 + luck × 0.025) ^ difficulty rank, from Auto (0) to Extreme Demon (10). Higher luck improves the relative chance of harder levels. Levels within a difficulty have equal weight.

Odds change as the pool grows. Ratings like Mythic are level metadata. This fan project is unaffiliated with RobTop Games.
